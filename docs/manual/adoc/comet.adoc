[[comet-commit]]
=== Reference orbit and commit
Location:: Comet › Reference orbit and commit
Shown:: no comet loaded

.Comet › Reference orbit and commit
Before the first commit the reference orbit is chosen here and the button is ready.

image::img/comet-commit--empty--light--tagged.png[Comet › Reference orbit and commit,width=507,pdfwidth=86mm]

. *Reference orbit* -- Chooses the two-body orbit drawn next to the true SPK path: the SBDB solution at its own epoch, or osculating at the start epoch. Required before the first commit.
. *Download & Commit* -- Downloads the selected SPK record and attaches it to the simulation with the proposed range.

==== Properties

Reference orbit::
  The red track is a two-body (Keplerian) reference orbit drawn next to the true SPK path.

  SBDB solution: the elements JPL publishes, osculating at the solution epoch, which can be years away from your window (67P: 2015-10-10, about 0.12 AU off its 2025 path because of Jupiter).

  Osculating at start epoch: elements recomputed from the SPK position and velocity at your start epoch. Exact there, and within about 10³ km over a month (planetary perturbations and outgassing forces).

  Required before the first commit; it cannot be changed while a comet is committed.

[[comet-simulated-range]]
=== Simulated range
Location:: Comet › Simulated range
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Comet › Simulated range
The range as committed: the date, the number of rotations and the resulting span in days.

image::img/comet-simulated-range--committed--light--tagged.png[Comet › Simulated range,width=571,pdfwidth=97mm]

. *Simulated range* -- Section for the observation date, the number of rotations before it, the dust lead time and Run at this date.
. *From image* -- Takes the observation date from the loaded FITS image.
. *Observation date (UTC)* -- Type the date of your telescope image in UTC. The simulated range ends at this date.
. *Rotations before it* -- Number of full nucleus rotations before the observation date at which the simulation starts, from 1 to 1000.
. *Span* -- Shows the length of the simulated range in days, from the number of rotations and the rotation period.
. *Dust lead time* -- Days before the start epoch at which the jets begin to emit, from 0 to 30. The default is 30, so the tail already exists at the start.
. *Run at this date* -- Sets the range from the observation date, commits the comet again and seeks to the date; with a FITS image it also sets up the comparison.
. *Run status* -- Shows what Run at this date is doing or what it did.
. *Advanced: Start / End, Restore, Recommit* -- Opens the hand-typed Start and End epochs, the Restore button and the range lock controls.

==== Properties

Dust lead time::
  Days the jets start emitting before the start epoch, from 0 to 30 (30 = one dust lifetime, the default).

  With 30 the tail is already fully formed when the timeline starts; with 0 the jets switch on at the start and the tail grows from nothing.

  Used the next time the comet is committed.

[[comet-range-advanced]]
=== Simulated range: Advanced
Location:: Comet › Simulated range
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Comet › Simulated range
The advanced part of the range lets you type the Start and End epochs by hand, restore the committed range, or set a range without running.

image::img/comet-range-advanced--committed--light--tagged.png[Comet › Simulated range,width=561,pdfwidth=95mm]

. *Advanced: Start / End, Restore, Recommit* -- Opens the hand-typed Start and End epochs, the Restore button and the range lock controls.
. *Start* -- Type the start epoch of the simulated range in ISO 8601 form, in the chosen time scale. It only proposes a range until you commit.
. *End* -- Type the end epoch of the simulated range in ISO 8601 form, in the chosen time scale. It must be at least 1 day after the start.
. *Restore* -- Puts the committed range back in the Start and End boxes and drops your edit.
. *Proposed* -- Shows the range in the Start and End boxes, which the next commit or recommit will use.
. *Set range* -- Applies the range from the observation date and rotation count without running the simulation.

==== Properties

Proposed range::
  The range in the Start/End boxes: what the next Download & Commit or Recommit will use.

  It does not change the running simulation. Restore puts the committed range back in the boxes.

[[comet-nucleus]]
=== Nucleus
Location:: Comet › Nucleus
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Comet › Nucleus
The Nucleus section holds the nucleus radius. The jets start on a sphere of this radius, so it must be known before you add jets.

image::img/comet-nucleus--committed--light--tagged.png[Comet › Nucleus,width=511,pdfwidth=87mm]

. *Nucleus* -- Section with the physical parameters of the nucleus, here its radius.
. *Nucleus Radius* -- Sets the radius of the nucleus in km, from 0.1 to 100; it is filled from Horizons when a comet is committed. The jets start on this sphere.

==== Properties

Nucleus Radius::
  Bounding-sphere radius of the comet nucleus, in km (0.1 to 100); the jets start on this sphere.

  Auto-filled from Horizons when a comet is committed. Override it here if needed.

  An edit after Play returns the simulation to the snapshot taken at Play.

[[comet-rotation]]
=== Rotational model
Location:: Comet › Rotational model
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Comet › Rotational model
Rotational Model sets the spin axis, the rotation phase and the spin rate of the nucleus. It is available after a comet is committed, and changes are applied live.

image::img/comet-rotation--committed--light--tagged.png[Comet › Rotational model,width=571,pdfwidth=97mm]

. *Rotational Model* -- Section with the spin axis, rotation phase and spin rate of the nucleus. It is available once a comet is committed.
. *Pole RA* -- Right ascension of the spin axis (J2000), in degrees. Together with Pole Dec it sets where the axis points.
. *Pole Dec* -- Declination of the spin axis (J2000), in degrees. Together with Pole RA it sets where the axis points.
. *Prime Meridian* -- Rotation angle of the nucleus at J2000, in degrees; it sets which side faces where at a given time.
. *Rotation Period (h)* -- Sidereal rotation period of the nucleus in hours. It changes the rotation rate below and the length of one rotation in the range.
. *Advanced: rates (precession, °/day)* -- Opens the precession rates of the spin axis and the rotation rate in degrees per day.
. *Rotation Rate (°/day)* -- Spin rate of the nucleus in degrees per day; the sign gives prograde or retrograde. It is the same spin as the period.
