[[main]]
=== Main window
Location:: Main window
Shown:: no comet loaded

.Main window
The main window of a new session: no comet is committed, so the viewport is empty and the Timeline only shows the range typed in the Comet tab.

image::img/main--empty--light--tagged.png[Main window,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Orbit* -- Shows the comet's orbit in the Solar System, animated over the chosen range.
. *Nucleus* -- Shows the nucleus with its orientation, the Sun direction, illumination and jets.
. *Model* -- Sets the view up to compare the model with a telescope image; the end date is the image date.
. *Settings* -- Tab with time scale, comet appearance, billboards, particle rendering, input bindings and viewport settings.
. *Comet* -- Tab to search and commit a comet, set the simulated range and nucleus radius, and edit the rotational model.
. *Model* -- Tab with the dust display options, the gizmo, the jets and the dust grain and activity defaults.
. *Viewport 3D* -- Tab with the 3D view of the simulation. There is only one, and it is pinned: it cannot be closed, replaced or dragged to another pane.
. *Imports* -- Tab that imports meshes (OBJ, PLY, glTF, GLB) and textures (PNG, JPEG, KTX2, FITS) and lists them. Textures embedded in a mesh are listed separately.
. *Legend* -- Explains the coloured lines in the viewport: in the Nucleus view the gizmo's grid, body axes, spin axis, Sun and velocity directions and one line per jet; in the Orbit and Model views the orbit lines. Its toggle collapses it.
. *Dust* -- Section with the dust display options, the gizmo, the jet list and the defaults for new jets.
. *Import…* -- Opens a file dialog to import meshes (OBJ, PLY, glTF, GLB) or textures (PNG, JPEG, KTX2).
. *Scale ruler* -- Shows the length in km that the bar beside it covers at the comet in the current view.
. *Timeline* -- Tab that shows the simulated range and, once a comet is committed, plays it: speed, reset, play or pause, run to end, and a box to jump to an epoch.
. *Start Epoch* -- Shows the start of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.
. *End Epoch* -- Shows the end of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.

[[main-committed]]
=== Main window
Location:: Main window
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Main window
The main window with a comet committed and the Model view active: the dust, the legend, the ephemeris table and the timeline controls are filled in.

image::img/main--committed--light--tagged.png[Main window,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Orbit* -- Shows the comet's orbit in the Solar System, animated over the chosen range.
. *Nucleus* -- Shows the nucleus with its orientation, the Sun direction, illumination and jets.
. *Model* -- Sets the view up to compare the model with a telescope image; the end date is the image date.
. *Time scale for dates* -- Section that chooses the time scale used for the dates you type and read.
. *Import…* -- Opens a file dialog to import meshes (OBJ, PLY, glTF, GLB) or textures (PNG, JPEG, KTX2).
. *Time scale* -- Chooses UTC or TDB for the Start and End boxes, the Timeline epoch box and the viewport epoch label. UTC is the default.
. *Comet Appearance* -- Section that sets how the nucleus is drawn. It changes the look only, not the simulation.
. *Billboards* -- Section to create billboard windows that show imported images over the desktop.
. *Texture* -- Chooses the imported texture to show in a new billboard window.
. *Centre (desktop px), X* -- Horizontal position of the billboard centre, in desktop pixels.
. *Centre (desktop px), Y* -- Vertical position of the billboard centre, in desktop pixels.
. *Size (px), width* -- Width of the billboard in pixels, at least 16.
. *Size (px), height* -- Height of the billboard in pixels, at least 16.
. *Keep aspect* -- When on, changing the width or the height also changes the other so the image keeps its proportions.
. *Opacity* -- Opacity of the billboard, from 0 (transparent) to 1 (opaque).

==== Properties

Texture::
  Imported texture to show in a new billboard window over the desktop; the button next to it opens the window. Import images in the Imports tab.

Keep aspect::
  When on, changing the width or height also changes the other, keeping the image proportions.

Opacity::
  Opacity of the billboard, from 0 (fully transparent) to 1 (fully opaque).

[[menu-file]]
=== File menu
Location:: Menu bar › File menu
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Menu bar › File menu
The File menu creates, opens and saves sessions. The screenshot lists its entries.

image::img/menu-file--committed--light--tagged.png[Menu bar › File menu,width=280,pdfwidth=47mm]

. *New* -- Starts a new, empty session. If the current one has unsaved changes, you are asked whether to save them first.
. *Open...* -- Opens a saved session (Ctrl+O): an .avk file, or the aethervk_index.json of a save folder. Asks about unsaved changes first.
. *Open Recent* -- Lists the last saves opened or written, up to 10; pick one to open it. Shows (none) when the list is empty.
. *Save* -- Saves the session to its current file (Ctrl+S). A session that was never saved asks for a file name, as Save As does.
. *Save As...* -- Saves the session to a new .avk file (Ctrl+Shift+S); the extension is added when missing.
. *Save As Folder...* -- Saves the session as a folder of separate files instead of one .avk file, for inspecting a save. Open reads it back through its aethervk_index.json.

[[docking]]
=== Docking and tabs
Location:: Docking and tabs
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Docking and tabs
Each pane of the window is a group of tabs. The tab strip has a button to add a tab, a button to swap the pane and a button to close it.

image::img/docking--committed--light--tagged.png[Docking and tabs,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Settings* -- Tab with time scale, comet appearance, billboards, particle rendering, input bindings and viewport settings.
. *Time scale for dates* -- Section that chooses the time scale used for the dates you type and read.
. *Comet Appearance* -- Section that sets how the nucleus is drawn. It changes the look only, not the simulation.
. *Time scale* -- Chooses UTC or TDB for the Start and End boxes, the Timeline epoch box and the viewport epoch label. UTC is the default.
. *Import…* -- Opens a file dialog to import meshes (OBJ, PLY, glTF, GLB) or textures (PNG, JPEG, KTX2).
. *Texture* -- Chooses the imported texture to show in a new billboard window.
. *Visible* -- Shows or hides the billboard window without deleting it.
. *Centre (desktop px), X* -- Horizontal position of the billboard centre, in desktop pixels.
. *Size (px), width* -- Width of the billboard in pixels, at least 16.
. *Keep aspect* -- When on, changing the width or the height also changes the other so the image keeps its proportions.
. *Opacity* -- Opacity of the billboard, from 0 (transparent) to 1 (opaque).
. *Rotation (°)* -- Rotation of the billboard about its centre, in degrees from -180 to 180.
. *Image height at the comet (km)* -- Physical height of the image at the comet's distance, in km. When set, it takes precedence over the plate scale.
. *Centre (desktop px), Y* -- Vertical position of the billboard centre, in desktop pixels.
. *Size (px), height* -- Height of the billboard in pixels, at least 16.

==== Properties

Texture::
  Imported texture to show in a new billboard window over the desktop; the button next to it opens the window. Import images in the Imports tab.

Keep aspect::
  When on, changing the width or height also changes the other, keeping the image proportions.

Opacity::
  Opacity of the billboard, from 0 (fully transparent) to 1 (fully opaque).

Rotation (°)::
  Rotation of the billboard about its centre, in degrees (−180 to 180). The rotation handle on the billboard does the same.

Image height at the comet (km)::
  The image's vertical extent at the comet, in kilometres (dot decimals), for an image without a WCS (a JPEG, for example). When set, it wins over the plate scale.

  Empty: not set.

=== Mode bar
Location:: Mode bar
Shown:: pending range edit, the range is locked, committed comet

.Mode bar
The mode bar after the range was edited while a comet is committed: a lock message and a Recommit button appear beside the mode buttons.

image::img/main-modebar--locked--light--tagged.png[Mode bar,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Mode hint* -- Shows a message after you choose Model mode: a hint to import a FITS image, or the result of the comparison set-up.
. *Range lock message* -- Appears when the range in the Comet tab differs from the committed one: the simulation keeps the committed range until you decommit, or recommit with the new range.
. *Recommit with this range* -- Commits the comet again, as Download & Commit does, with the range typed in the Comet tab; the dust restarts from the new start. Shown only while the range is locked.
. *Orbit* -- Shows the comet's orbit in the Solar System, animated over the chosen range.
. *Nucleus* -- Shows the nucleus with its orientation, the Sun direction, illumination and jets.
. *Model* -- Sets the view up to compare the model with a telescope image; the end date is the image date.
