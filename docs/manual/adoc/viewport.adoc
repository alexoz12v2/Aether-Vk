[[overlay]]
=== Viewport overlay
Location:: Viewport overlay
Shown:: Orbit mode, committed comet

.Viewport overlay
The overlay in Orbit mode: the orbit legend, the ephemeris table, the scale ruler and the epoch label.

image::img/overlay--orbit--light--tagged.png[Viewport overlay,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Scale ruler* -- Shows the length in km that the bar beside it covers at the comet in the current view.
. *Epoch* -- Shows the current simulation epoch in the chosen time scale.
. *Reference frame* -- Describes the view, for example from Earth with celestial north up, and repeats the epoch.
