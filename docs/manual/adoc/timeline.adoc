Shown:: no comet loaded

.Timeline

. *Start Epoch* -- Shows the start of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.
. *End Epoch* -- Shows the end of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.

[[timeline-committed]]
=== Timeline
Location:: Timeline
Shown:: committed comet (67P, December 2021)

.Timeline
After the commit the Timeline shows the committed range, the speed selector, the playback buttons, the progress bar and the current epoch.

image::img/timeline--committed--light--tagged.png[Timeline,width=900,pdfwidth=170mm]

. *Timeline* -- Tab that shows the simulated range and, once a comet is committed, plays it: speed, reset, play or pause, run to end, and a box to jump to an epoch.
. *2021-12-25T11:00:59 to 2021-12-27T00:14:47* -- The range the simulation runs: the comet's ephemeris covers it, and Play, Reset, Run to End and Go stay inside it.
. *Start Epoch* -- Shows the start of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.
. *End Epoch* -- Shows the end of the simulated range in the chosen time scale. It is read-only here; change it in the Comet tab.
. *Speed* -- Chooses how fast simulated time runs while playing, as simulated time per real second, for example 1 h/s.
. *Epoch* -- Shows the current epoch; type an epoch inside the committed range and press Enter or Go to jump there.
. *Reset* -- Goes back to the start epoch of the committed range and rebuilds the dust there. It is hidden while playing.
. *Play / Pause* -- Starts the simulation at the chosen speed, or pauses it and keeps the current epoch.
. *Go* -- Jumps to the epoch typed in the Epoch box, within the committed range.
. *Run to End* -- Jumps to the end epoch of the committed range without animating; the dust is rebuilt there.

==== Properties

Committed range::
  The range the simulation runs: the comet's ephemeris covers it, and Play, Reset, Run to End and Go stay inside it.

Playback speed::
  How fast simulated time runs while playing, as simulated time per real second (for example 1 h/s: one simulated hour per second).

Current epoch::
  The simulation's current epoch, in the time scale shown in the label.

  Type an epoch inside the committed range and press Enter, or Go, to jump there; the dust is rebuilt at that epoch.
